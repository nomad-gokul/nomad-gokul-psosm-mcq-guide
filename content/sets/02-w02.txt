N: Echo chamber = social (like-minded people reinforce each other). Filter bubble = algorithmic (personalized feeds narrow what you see). Filter bubbles often feed echo chambers.
N: Degree = direct links (local reach). Betweenness = bridges between communities. Closeness = 1 / average distance. Eigenvector / PageRank = links from important nodes count more.
N: Local clustering coefficient = links among a node's neighbours ÷ k(k−1)/2. Small-world = high clustering + short paths. Scale-free = power-law degree distribution (few hubs).
N: Hurricane Sandy fake-image study: Decision Tree ~97% accuracy; tweet-based features worked, user-based features were poor.
